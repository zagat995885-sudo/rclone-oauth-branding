rclone-drive

This is a personal, single-user OAuth application owned and operated by the Google account holder (zagat995885@gmail.com). It exists solely to let the owner run rclone on their own computer against their own Google Drive.

What the app does

  • Syncs, copies and lists files in the owner's own Google Drive, initiated by the owner from their own machine.
  • Uses the Google Drive API scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly.
  • Has no server component, no other users, no analytics and no third-party data sharing.
